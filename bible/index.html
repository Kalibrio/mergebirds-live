0 · North star

We sell the feeling of digging a perfect tunnel and watching luck agree with your plan.

Every merge game gives the player placement. Merge Birds gives them excavation: you never touch the birds — you sculpt the terrain, and gravity plays your move. That indirection reads as a physics toy, plans like a puzzle, and resolves like a slot machine settling.

Pinned rules — violate these and the feature dies in review: (1) nothing may ever bypass the dig; (2) no timers, tension lives in space (the rising water, the pile); (3) money never buys a merged bird; (4) celebrations never block play.

1 · The core loop

A round: three birds (a trio) glide onto the fresh sand block. The player carves tunnels with a finger; birds fall; same-tier birds that touch merge into the next tier (attract → pop → bloom, 0.18 s attract, upward kick). When every trio bird is in play — reached the pool, merged away, or sunk below the start line into contact with another bird — the sand regrows around survivors and the next trio arrives, costing 1 move. Levels are won by their goal (§4) and lost by overflow: the settled pile or the rising waterline breaching the lose line for 2.5 s.

  • Digging is free — moves are consumed per round, so carving is play, not spend.
  • Merges relieve pressure — two birds displace more water than their merged child, so the waterline visibly drops on a merge. Tension and relief share one mechanic.
  • Undermining is a tactic — unsupported sand cascades (§2), so digging below can deliver birds a player couldn't reach directly.

6 · Game modes

Campaign

40 authored levels on the Journey map (stars, replays, goal markers). Stars: 3★ at 100% of the level's hand-tuned score target, 2★ at 55%, 1★ at 22%.

Daily Dig

One date-seeded endless pit per day — same rocks, TNT, stone egg, and theme for every player. No moves, no goals; overflow ends it; score banks as coins. Per-day best is tracked and shareable. Weekly modifiers rotate: classic · low-gravity (0.9) · all-clay (clay from 12% depth) · lemon-rain (free lemon every 40 s). First dig of each day pays +2💎.

Bird Race (tournament)

Every level is an async race: your live target-progress vs ghost curves of your own past runs (sampled every 5 s, last 4 kept) plus synthetic pacers until history exists. Standings re-rank each second; placement pays 50/30/20/10/10 bonus coins. Daily window ends at local midnight. Ghost share links (?ghost=code) inject a friend's run as an opponent — viral, zero backend.

7 · Meta & retention

  • Daily missions — 3 parallel, date-seeded from a pool of 6 (merge 20 · dig 600 · collect 6 targets · combo ×4 · win 3 · fever ×2). Auto-claimed micro-rewards via non-blocking banners. Weekly goal: win 12 → 800🪙.
  • Win streaks — 2: chest +100🪙 · 3: +1💣 · 5: +300🪙 +5💎. Real fails reset; the near-win rescue does not.
  • Migration Pass — free monthly track, 10 nodes at 50 pts each. Points: win 10 + 2×stars (+5 for 1st place); Daily Dig up to 20. Final node pays 15💎.
  • Aviary (Bird Album) — tap a bird, it sings its motif note; rarity borders; set-completion bonuses 100/250/500/1000🪙 (+20💎 Legendary); Goldie quest meter + dated statue; nest decoration unlocks at 10★/25★ totals.
  • Session pacing — >15 level-starts per hour earns exactly one friendly stretch nudge per hour. No hard limits, no reward nerfs.

9 · Difficulty science

  • Scripted first session — L1's opening trio is [Chick, Chick, Sunny]: the first fall produces the first merge. Tutorial hand until first dig. Gift bomb before L3.
  • Quiet assist — 2 consecutive fails on a level flattens the spawner (exponent 1.6→1.0) and allows one tier higher. Invisible; resets on a win.
  • Near-win rescue — failing exactly 1 short of the goal offers +2 free moves, once per attempt.
  • Instrumentation — ?stats=1: per-level starts/wins/fails with ⚠ flags under 40% winrate, plus a 15 s gameplay clip recorder (the ad-creative factory). Events also queryable via __mbEvents().

10 · Feel & audio identity

  • Merge choreography — attract (pull + inflate + lean-in) → pop (flash, double ring, tier-colored burst, stars) → bloom (elastic overshoot). Impact squash on every hard contact; 120 Hz fixed-substep physics.
  • The motif — everything musical is major pentatonic on C5. Merge chimes climb by tier and combo; each bird's Aviary voice is its tier's note; the Goldie chorus is the whole scale. The motif is the audio logo.
  • Two-stem music — calm dig loop (pad + sparse plucks) and fever layer (16th arpeggio + hats) on separate buses, crossfaded by live game intensity (fever, combos, danger). Fully synthesized; zero audio assets.
  • Premium rendering — ×3 supersampled terrain, contact shadows, vignette, biome backdrops (day clouds / sunset sun / night stars + moon), golden-hour and fever full-screen states.

12 · Tech & ops

  • Stack — TypeScript + Vite + matter-js + canvas; DOM overlay UI; WebAudio synth. No other dependencies, no audio/art pipeline.
  • Ship shape — one self-contained ~630 KB index.html (sprites inlined) + manifest + icon. PWA: add-to-home-screen runs full-screen.
  • Deploy — build → singlefile → push to Kalibrio/mergebirds-live → GitHub Pages serves mergebirds.kalibrio.com (HTTPS enforced). ~1 minute to live.
  • Saves — all local: merge-birds-save-v1 (progress, currencies, album, ghosts, streaks), mb-missions-v1, mb-tuning-v1, mb-events-v1. Migration = spread-over-defaults; add fields, never rename.

13 · Expansion map

Ready lanes (client-only)

  • More grammar — the material/entity system takes new pieces cheaply: geysers that launch birds, moving rock platforms, sandstorm rounds, multi-chamber pits.
  • Cosmetics depth — dig-brush trails, bird skins, pit themes; sinks for stars and gems; Pass becomes cosmetic-heavy.
  • Events — the Daily Dig seed + modifier system generalizes to weekend events and themed weeks without new mechanics.
  • Sprite-sheet micro-life — true blinks/cheek-puffs when bird art gets a second pass.

Blocked lanes (need outside inputs)

  • Backend tier (AWS creds): real leaderboard divisions, Flocks/clubs, cross-device saves, analytics collection, kill-gate measurement (D1 ≥ 40%, D7 ≥ 20%, D30 ≥ 10%).
  • Native wrapper (Xcode): Taptic haptics, Game Center, push, App Store.
  • Monetization SDKs: rewarded ads into the rescue slot; IAP selling gems.

When adding anything, re-read §0. The dig is the game; everything else is an amplifier.
